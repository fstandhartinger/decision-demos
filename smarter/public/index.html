    Method and honesty

    • Published numbers. The Capability Score and the category cells come from the published JevBench v1.6.27 files (17 Oct 2026, draw v1.6-regular-20261012, 1,500 items, 1,200 sealed). s1-pro is served as s1-pro-3 (vLLM-SR d3 26B). Category values are chance-corrected competence and are not part of the JevBench Score.
    • Our own run. 17 Oct 2026 on the public JevBench v1.6.1 set (300 open items). Three over-long items fail on every model, so 297 are compared. The request format is identical for both models, from the same server, one question per request. Jev answers on this page are recorded; no live Jev calls are made.
    • Top-1 is a simple metric. Accuracy and log loss here are not the official JevBench score. They are easy to check, and they say less than the published score.
    • Check it yourself. These are public JevBench items (open split). All 297 are browsable above, including those where s1-pro is wrong. The full board is at benchmarkheaven.com/jev-models.

    Jev is a model by TypeSafe. "Jev-compatible" only describes the format of our API; our models are not Jev. For speed see s1-fast.
