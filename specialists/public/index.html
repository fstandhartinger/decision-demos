A. s1-llm-auto-router

A router classifier for LLM gateways. For one user request it answers seven typed questions in a single call: what kind of request it is, how hard, how high the stakes, whether it needs tools, vision or a long context, and whether it is a follow-up. It runs on CPU, about 26 ms inside the model.

Where the difference comes from

Difficulty and stakes: exact level matched (difficulty 5 levels, stakes 4 levels). Flags: yes/no accuracy at p ≥ 0.5. Category calibration error (ECE): how far stated confidence is from actual accuracy, lower is better.

B. Demand forecasting and risk assessment

Method and honesty

  • Router held-out set.
  • Same request for both. Jev answers on this page are our own recorded measurements from ; no live Jev calls are made.
  • Labels are not perfect. The gold answers come from an ensemble of LLM labellers, not from humans. Where the two models disagree with each other, look at the item and judge for yourself.
  • Offline models. The demand-forecasting and risk-assessment models in section B were measured offline on our GPU. They are not yet available on api.decisionmodels.io. Only the router is live.

Jev is a model by TypeSafe. "Jev-compatible" only describes the format of our API; our models are not Jev. For the general comparison see Smarter.
